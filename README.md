WordVaults helps you build a personal vocabulary collection while browsing the web.

When you discover an unfamiliar, useful, or interesting word, simply activate WordVaults, select the word or phrase, and save it to your collection. You can continue browsing while the floating toolbar stays accessible on screen.

FEATURES

• Select words and phrases directly from webpages
• Save vocabulary with a simple click or keyboard shortcut
• Capture the surrounding context of selected words
• Store the source URL and webpage title
• Keep your vocabulary collection organized
• Search saved words and phrases
• Mark important words as favorites
• Detect duplicate vocabulary entries
• Edit and delete saved vocabulary
• Export your collection as CSV
• Use a floating toolbar while browsing and scrolling
• Use keyboard shortcuts for faster vocabulary collection
• Store vocabulary locally in the extension

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open a webpage.
2. Activate WordVaults.
3. Select an unfamiliar or useful word or phrase.
4. Save it to your vocabulary collection.
5. Continue browsing and collecting words.
6. Open your collection to review your saved vocabulary.
7. Export your vocabulary as CSV whenever you need it.

WHO IS IT FOR?

WordVaults is useful for students, language learners, researchers, writers, developers, and anyone who regularly reads articles, documentation, research papers, or other online content.

Instead of copying words into scattered notes, screenshots, or documents, WordVaults lets you collect them directly from the webpages where you discover them.

Build your personal vocabulary naturally while you browse.
