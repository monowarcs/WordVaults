WordVaults Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

WordVaults is a Chrome extension that helps users collect, organize, search, and export useful or unfamiliar words and phrases encountered while browsing the web.

1. Information WordVaults Processes

WordVaults processes information that the user explicitly selects or saves while using the extension. Depending on the features used, this may include:

  • Selected words or phrases.
  • Context surrounding a selected word or phrase.
  • Webpage title and source URL associated with a saved item.
  • Notes, favorites, and other vocabulary information entered by the user.
  • Date and time associated with saved vocabulary items.

2. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to provide WordVaults' core functionality, including:

  • Saving vocabulary selected by the user.
  • Displaying and organizing saved vocabulary.
  • Searching the user's vocabulary collection.
  • Preserving context and source information.
  • Allowing the user to edit, delete, and favorite saved items.
  • Exporting the user's vocabulary collection as CSV.

3. Local Storage

WordVaults uses Chrome extension storage to persist the user's vocabulary collection. Vocabulary data is stored locally in the user's browser.

WordVaults does not require a remote account or backend service for its core vocabulary-storage functionality.

4. Data Sharing

WordVaults does not sell user data and does not transfer saved vocabulary, webpage content, or browsing information to third parties for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

WordVaults does not intentionally collect or share passwords, authentication credentials, payment information, health information, personal communications, or precise location information.

5. Webpage Access

WordVaults accesses the active webpage when the user activates the extension so that it can provide its vocabulary-selection and vocabulary-capture functionality.

The extension is designed to capture content relevant to the user's explicit vocabulary-selection action rather than continuously collecting the contents of websites for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Security

WordVaults does not operate a server-side database for the core vocabulary collection. Users should protect access to their browser and device because locally stored extension data is subject to the security of the user's browser and device.

7. Data Deletion

Users can delete saved vocabulary through the WordVaults interface. Removing the extension also removes its extension storage according to Chrome's extension storage behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

WordVaults is not specifically directed at children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when WordVaults' functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For questions or support regarding this privacy policy or WordVaults, please use the support contact or support page provided with the WordVaults Chrome Web Store listing.
